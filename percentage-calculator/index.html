Percentage Calculator

The most common percentage question: what is X% of Y? Enter the percentage and the value to get the result, the remainder, and the working. Handy for tips, commissions, exam marks and discounts.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Enter the percentage.
  2. Enter the value it applies to.
  3. Select “Calculate”.

Formula and method

Result = (percentage ÷ 100) × value. The remainder shown is value − result.

Example

15% of 240 is 36. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 36 ÷ 240 × 100 = 15%.

Can the percentage be over 100?

Yes. 150% of 240 is 360 — the result is simply larger than the starting value.
