Discount Calculator

See the final price and exactly how much you save for any percentage-off deal. Add a sales-tax rate to get the true out-the-door price after discount and tax.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Optionally enter a tax rate, then calculate.

Formula and method

Savings = price × discount% ÷ 100. Sale price = price − savings. If tax is entered, tax is applied to the discounted price (the common retail practice).

Example

A $120 item at 25% off costs $90 — you save $30. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

How do stacked discounts work?

Apply them one at a time: 20% off then 10% off a $100 item gives $72, not $70 — the second discount applies to the already reduced price.

Is tax charged before or after a discount?

In most retail settings, manufacturer coupons reduce the taxable price while store discounts may vary by jurisdiction. This tool applies tax after the discount.
