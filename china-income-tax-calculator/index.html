China Income Tax Calculator (Income Tax + Social Insurance)

What actually lands in your bank account from a Chinese salary depends on three things: your personal social insurance and housing fund contributions （五险一金）, the 5,000-yuan monthly standard deduction plus any special additional deductions （专项附加扣除）, and the cumulative withholding method （累计预扣法） that makes monthly tax rise as the year progresses. This calculator models all three: pick a city preset (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Hangzhou, Chengdu) or enter your own contribution bases and rates, and see the month-by-month withholding, the annual tax and the annual take-home. It also works backwards — enter the take-home pay you want and it solves for the gross salary required.

How to use the China Income Tax Calculator (Income Tax + Social Insurance)

  1. Choose a city preset, or switch to custom parameters.
  2. Enter your monthly gross salary — or switch to reverse mode and enter the take-home pay you want.
  3. Add your monthly special additional deductions and housing fund rate, then calculate.

Formula and method

Monthly personal contributions = contribution base (your salary clamped to the local min/max) × personal rates (pension 8%, medical 2%, unemployment 0.5% — 0.2% in Guangdong — plus the housing fund at your employer’s 5–12%). Cumulative taxable income for month m = m × (gross − 5,000 − contributions − special deductions); cumulative tax uses the annual brackets (3% to 36,000, then 10%/20%/25%/30%/35%/45% with quick deductions); each month withholds the increase over what was already withheld.

Example

At a 30,000-yuan monthly salary with 4,500 yuan of monthly contributions and 2,000 yuan of special deductions, January withholding is 555 yuan and February is 625 yuan — the worked example pattern published with the cumulative withholding rules. Frequently asked questions

Why does my monthly tax go up during the year?

Cumulative withholding taxes your year-to-date income. Early months sit in the 3% bracket; as cumulative income crosses 36,000 and 144,000 yuan, higher rates apply to later months, so the same salary is withheld more heavily towards year-end. The annual total is what matters.

Are the city parameters official?

Each preset names its source announcement and the date we last checked it (Beijing and Shanghai use the July-2026 bases; Guangdong uses the province-wide pension base). Bases and rates change yearly and employers choose the housing fund rate, so treat results as estimates and edit the parameters to match your own payslip.

Does it handle year-end bonuses or multiple employers?

No. It models a single employer paying a constant monthly salary for a full year. Bonuses taxed separately, mid-year job changes and annual settlement （汇算清缴） refunds or top-ups are outside its scope.

Is my salary data stored?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded or saved.
