BMR Calculator

Your basal metabolic rate is the energy your body uses at complete rest. This calculator applies the Mifflin–St Jeor equation — currently the most widely recommended estimate — from your age, sex, weight and height.

Health figures on this page are general information only and are not medical advice.

How to use the BMR Calculator

  1. Choose metric or imperial.
  2. Enter sex, age, weight and height.
  3. Select “Calculate”.

Formula and method

Men: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5. Women: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age − 161. Results are kilocalories per day.

Example

A 30-year-old man at 70 kg and 175 cm has a BMR of about 1,649 kcal/day. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

How is BMR different from TDEE?

BMR is rest-only energy. Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) multiplies BMR by an activity factor — the calorie calculator does that for you.

How accurate is the equation?

It estimates population averages; individual values can differ by roughly ±10%. Indirect calorimetry is the clinical measurement.
