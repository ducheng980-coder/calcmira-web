Average Calculator

Paste a list of numbers — separated by commas, spaces or new lines — to get the mean (average), median, mode, range, sum and count in one pass. Useful for grades, prices, measurements and quick data checks.

How to use the Average Calculator

  1. Enter your numbers, separated by commas or spaces.
  2. Select “Calculate”.
  3. Compare the mean and median — a big gap means outliers are pulling the average.

Formula and method

Mean = sum ÷ count. Median is the middle value of the sorted list (or the mean of the two middle values). Mode is the most frequent value. Geometric mean is the n-th root of the product, defined here for positive values.

Example

For 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42: the mean is 18 and the median is 15.5. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

When is the median better than the mean?

When a few very large or small values (outliers) would distort the mean — incomes and house prices are classic examples.

What if every number appears once?

Then there is no mode, and the tool says so instead of inventing one.
