How to use the Reading Time Calculator

  1. Paste the full text into the box.
  2. Read the estimated reading and speaking times.
  3. For a speech, rehearse once — your personal pace is the best calibration.

Formula and method

Reading time = word count ÷ 200 words per minute. Speaking time = word count ÷ 130 words per minute. Both are population averages, not personal measurements.

Example

A 1,000-word article takes about 5 minutes to read and about 7–8 minutes to read aloud. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Why 200 words per minute?

Research on adult reading rates puts average silent reading of non-fiction near 200–250 wpm; 200 is a conservative, widely used figure.

How long is a 10-minute speech?

At 130 wpm, about 1,300 words. Leave pauses for emphasis and audience reaction.
