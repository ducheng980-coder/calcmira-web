Age Calculator

Enter a date of birth to get an exact age — years, months and days — plus total weeks and days lived and the countdown to the next birthday. Add an “age at date” to work out how old someone was or will be on any specific day, for forms, school enrolment or eligibility checks.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Optionally set the “age at date” (defaults to today).
  3. Select “Calculate” to see the full breakdown.

Formula and method

Age is computed on the real calendar: full years first, then full months, then remaining days — the same convention official forms use. A person born on 29 February is treated as having their birthday on 28 February or 1 March in non-leap years depending on jurisdiction; this tool follows the calendar arithmetic of the entered dates.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my age differ by a day from another calculator?

Some tools count with fixed 30-day months or include both endpoints. This one uses exact calendar arithmetic, which matches official documents.

Can I calculate the age of a historical figure?

Yes — set the “age at date” to the date of death (or any date) to get the age at that moment.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
