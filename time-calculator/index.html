Time Calculator

What time is it 2 hours 45 minutes after 21:30? Add or subtract a duration from a start time and get the resulting clock time, including how many days forward or back it lands.

Advertisement

How to use the Time Calculator

  1. Enter a start time as HH:MM or HH:MM:SS.
  2. Choose Add or Subtract.
  3. Enter a duration in the same format and calculate.

Formula and method

Times are converted to seconds, added or subtracted, then converted back to a 24-hour clock time; whole days of overflow are reported separately.

Example

21:30 + 2:45 = 00:15 the next day. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Can the duration be longer than 24 hours?

Yes. Durations up to 999 hours are accepted, and the day shift is shown next to the result.

Does it handle AM/PM?

Enter times in 24-hour format (for example 14:30 for 2:30 PM) to avoid ambiguity.
