Loan Calculator

Estimate the monthly payment on a fixed-rate loan from the amount, annual interest rate and term. You also get total interest and the remaining balance at the end of each year — the numbers that decide whether a loan is affordable.

Financial figures on this page are estimates for planning only and are not financial advice.

How to use the Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate (APR, excluding fees).
  3. Enter the term in years and calculate.

Formula and method

Monthly payment M = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n is the number of monthly payments. With a 0% rate, M = P ÷ n.

Example

A $20,000 loan at 6% over 5 years costs about $386.66 per month and $3,199.36 in total interest. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include fees, insurance or taxes?

No. It models principal and interest only. Real loans may add origination fees, insurance and taxes that raise the true cost.

What happens if I pay extra each month?

Extra payments cut the balance faster and can save substantial interest. This estimator assumes the standard schedule; ask the lender for an amortisation table with extra payments.
