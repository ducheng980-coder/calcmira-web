Enter two fractions and an operation. The result is simplified to lowest terms and also shown as a decimal and a mixed number — the three forms schoolwork and recipes actually need.

How to use the Fraction Calculator

  1. Enter the first fraction’s numerator and denominator.
  2. Pick an operation.
  3. Enter the second fraction and calculate.

Formula and method

Addition and subtraction use a common denominator (a/b ± c/d = (ad ± bc)/bd). Multiplication is (a×c)/(b×d); division is (a×d)/(b×c). Results are reduced by the greatest common divisor.

Example

1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6 ≈ 0.833333. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Why must denominators be whole numbers and non-zero?

A fraction a/b is defined for integers a and b with b ≠ 0. Use the percentage calculator for decimal proportions.

How do I enter a mixed number like 1½?

Convert it to an improper fraction first: 1½ = 3/2. Or calculate the whole and fractional parts separately and add.
