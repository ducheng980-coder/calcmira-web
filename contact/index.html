Contact

The fastest way to reach us is by email. We read every message, and calculation-error reports are treated as bugs.

  • Calculation errors: tell us the tool name, the exact inputs, the result you got and the result you expected.
  • New tool suggestions: tell us the calculation and a real situation where you needed it.
  • Privacy questions: see the Privacy Policy first — the short version is that tool inputs never leave your browser.
  • Business and advertising: include your organisation and what you propose.

Email: contact@example.com (replace with the production mailbox once the domain is live — the site currently runs as a pre-launch build).

We do not offer phone support and we never ask for passwords, payment details or personal documents by email.
