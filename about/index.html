About CalcMira

CalcMira is a free library of online calculators and text tools for everyday questions: How old am I exactly? What is 15% of 240? Is this JSON valid? How much will this loan really cost?

How we work

  • Local by design. Every calculation runs in your browser with JavaScript. We operate no database of user inputs, and the tools need no account.
  • Transparent methods. Each page publishes its formula, a worked example and answers to common questions, so you can check the math yourself.
  • Honest limits. Health and finance pages are labelled as general information, not professional advice, and state their assumptions.

How the site is funded

The site is free to use. We plan to fund hosting and development with clearly separated display advertising (for example through Google AdSense) once the site qualifies. Advertising never sits inside a calculator, never covers results, and is never disguised as content. Until advertising is approved and live, pages show a neutral placeholder where an ad would appear.

Contact

See the Contact page for how to reach us about errors, suggestions or partnerships. Found a wrong result? Tell us the tool, the inputs and what you expected — reproducible reports get fixed first.
