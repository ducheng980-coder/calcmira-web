How to use the Paragraph Counter

  1. Paste your structured text into the box.
  2. Read the paragraph count and supporting totals.
  3. Add or merge paragraphs and check again.

Formula and method

A paragraph is a block of non-empty text separated from the next block by at least one line break. Empty lines are ignored.

Frequently asked questions

Does a single line break start a new paragraph?

Yes. Any line break separates paragraphs in this counter, which matches how most pasted drafts are structured.

Can I count paragraphs in a document file?

Copy the text out of the document and paste it here — the tool works on plain text.
